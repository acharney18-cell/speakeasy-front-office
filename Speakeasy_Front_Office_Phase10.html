Phase 4 · Championship Intelligence
Historical contender index combines season scoring (50%), last-three completed-week scoring (30%), and the weekly lineup's optimal scoring potential (20%). Each component is normalized to the league leader; missing components are omitted and the remaining weights are renormalized. This is a descriptive index, not projected championship odds. It does not adjust for injuries, future schedules, byes, or player projections.

Every completed week, each team is compared against all nine other teams. A win earns one all-play win, a tie earns half a win. Only fully reported weeks with all 10 roster scores are counted; incomplete or unavailable weeks are excluded. These are historical results, not projections.
