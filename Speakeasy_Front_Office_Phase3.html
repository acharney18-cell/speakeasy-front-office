What this can and cannot tell us

This evaluates past lineup decisions, not championship odds. Weekly matchup rosters and player scores come from Sleeper. We only calculate when the player directory supplies enough position eligibility and the weekly data supports a complete legal lineup. Future championship projections require a separate, verified forward-looking projection source.
Pick ownership is calculated from original round-by-round ownership plus Sleeper's traded-pick records. It includes future seasons only and may not reflect compensatory picks.
